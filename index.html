About Our Guild

Nurturing Tomorrow's Software Engineers & Innovators

The CPI ICT Club was established with a singular vision: to empower diploma engineering students with modern industry-grade technology skills, competitive coding instincts, and collaborative open-source mindsets.

Industry-Aligned Skills

Hands-on practice with modern stacks: Full Stack Web, Python, Cloud, UI/UX, and Cybersecurity.

Peer Mentorship

Senior club members mentor junior batches in programming, debugging, and project building.

Hackathons & Contests

Regular intra-college and national competitive programming contests with exciting prizes.

Career Acceleration

Direct job and internship placements, resume reviews, and portfolio architecture coaching.

Our Mission

To cultivate an inclusive, energetic ecosystem where every student receives the guidance, resources, and collaborative platform to transform theoretical knowledge into functional software solutions.

Our Vision

To establish CPI as a premier technological hub in Bangladesh by producing skilled software engineers, innovative entrepreneurs, and ethical technologists who lead global digital transformation.

Club Achievements & Legacy

Winner of 3 National Polytechnic Hackathons, 12+ student startup incubations, 600+ alumni working in top IT firms, and over 1,800 active community learners.
